Our Story

Welcome to Re-Loved Designs.

Founded by Courtney Rosenberg, Re-Loved Designs is a testament to the beauty of handmade craftsmanship and the joy of shared experiences.

What started as a passion for creative reuse and repurposing has blossomed into a vibrant studio space in Branford, Connecticut. Here, we believe that art isn't just for 'artists'—it's for everyone looking to express themselves, unwind, and create something beautiful.

Our philosophy is simple: foster a welcoming environment, provide inspiring materials, and let creativity flow. Whether you're painting, pouring resin, or stringing beads, the experience of making is just as important as the final product.
